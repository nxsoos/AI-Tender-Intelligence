# AI Tender Intelligence design direction

- **Product:** Help bid teams understand tender obligations and prepare complete submissions.
- **Character:** Precise, composed, accountable.
- **World:** Procurement review desk: paper, ink, annotations, and evidence.
- **Moments:** Actual checklist progress and critical obligations.
- **Signature:** Page-reference links beside sourced findings and assistant answers.

Use neutral paper surfaces (#F7F8F6 / #FFF), ink (#172321), restrained deep teal (#173F3A), and semantic risk colors. IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Arabic provide matched typography. Use a 4/8px spacing grid, 8px controls, 12px panels, and Hugeicons. The two-screen workspace follows an original document-first composition, not the assessment reference branding or screenshots.

Arabic mirrors the full layout through logical CSS properties. Source quotations remain verbatim; mixed strings are isolated with bdi, dates use Gregorian calendar and Western digits. No tracked Arabic letters, decorative gradients, invented metrics, fake answers, or inactive placeholder controls.

Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)), [heydamir/unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), CC BY 4.0, informed the spacing, color, icons, copy, typography, layout, details, and motion review.
